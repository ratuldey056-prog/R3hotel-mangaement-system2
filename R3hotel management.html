Manage Your Hotel Smarter.

⚠️ Demo Version Disclaimer

R3hotelmanagement is currently a demonstration/prototype software. This version is made for learning, testing and portfolio presentation. It is not a production-ready hotel management system and should not be used to manage real hotel operations, payments, employees or customer information.

Some features may contain errors or limitations because this is a single-file frontend demo. Data is stored locally in the browser for demonstration purposes. A more complete and genuinely useful version with a proper backend, database, secure authentication and reliable real-world functionality is planned for the future.

About R3hotelmanagement

A complete hotel operations prototype.

Everything in one place

R3hotelmanagement brings everyday hotel management tasks into one clean dashboard. Managers can monitor rooms, staff, housekeeping, inventory, salaries, supplier contacts and purchasing requirements.

The Shop Management module also tracks shop staff, products, stock shortages and customer suggestions for new products.
